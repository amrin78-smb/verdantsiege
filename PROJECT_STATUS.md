### Known issues / limitations
- **No real-device run yet.** FPS, thermals, touch feel, audio mix and the 32-bit Redmi A3 are unverified.
- Heartroot model is large; its canopy sits under the card rail on the left. Composition pass planned for M2.
- Wave flags slightly overlap the wave label on the progress bar (cosmetic).
- Tutorial is just three hint toasts (M2 delivers a proper tutorial).
- Cards are the default 3-card roster; no pre-battle roster selection yet.
- Ground tiles are flat colour; no sunlight pools (Verdant Heartlands feature, M2).
- The APK is debug-signed (no release keystore).
- Quality Low/Medium/High differ in render scale, MSAA and particle budget only; no profiling-based tuning yet.
