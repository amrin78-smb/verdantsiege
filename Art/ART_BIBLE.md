# Verdant Siege — Art Bible (Milestone 1, Verdant Heartlands)

All 3D art is **generated by project scripts** in `Tools/Blender/` (Blender 5.2, bpy + bmesh). Edit the
generator, never the exported file. Regenerate everything with `Tools/Blender/build_all.sh`.

## Look
Premium stylised low-poly: chunky readable silhouettes, strong colour blocking, deliberate mix of faceted
(stone, foliage clouds, plates) and smooth (stems, bodies, caps) shading. No textures; all colour is baked
per face-corner, with gradients (dark base → light tips) and small per-face value variation for a handcrafted feel.
Glow is reserved for **gameplay meaning**: spores, eyes, the Heartroot core, pickups, projectiles, weak points.

- **Defenders (living plants)**: warm, friendly, tactile. Petals, layered leaves, moss, glowing spores.
  Gentle faces at most (closed-eye arcs, peeking eyes). No googly cartoon faces.
- **The Blight (Rotbound)**: corrupted fungal/rot creatures. Dark plum/maroon/indigo bodies, lime and magenta
  glow, bone and bark armour. Each enemy has one dominant glow cue readable at phone size.
- **Verdant Heartlands**: lush greens, warm sandstone, mossy tops, sunlit.

## Proportions
- Battlefield cell 1.0 × 1.0 m. Defenders fit a 0.8 m footprint, 0.6–1.0 m tall.
- Enemies 0.6–1.4 m tall (Skitter is a deliberately low 0.6 m runner), Plated Brute 1.4 m and 0.98 m wide (stays inside one lane).
- Heartroot 3.5 m tall, roots span 4.7 m across the lanes (Y), 2.2 m deep (X).

## Technical conventions (contract with Unity — do not change without updating the game code)
- Metres, Z up (Blender). Root EMPTY named exactly as the model at (0,0,0) = ground centre of footprint.
  FX (`SeedBolt`, `Sunmote`) have their origin at the object **centre** (they fly / float).
- Facing: defenders **+X**, enemies **−X**, projectile flies **+X**. Verified in Unity: Blender +X → Unity +X.
- One face-corner BYTE colour attribute **`Col`** (sRGB). **Alpha = emission mask** (1 = self-lit glow, 0 = lit).
  The Unity shader must convert vertex RGB from sRGB to linear when the project is in Linear colour space.
- One material `VS_Vertex` on every mesh (Unity replaces it with the shared vertex-colour shader).
- Animatable parts are separate mesh children with their **origin at the joint**; transforms are clean (no
  rotation/scale). Parts per model are listed in `Tools/Blender/model_specs.json` and checked by `check_models.py`.
- Normals are authored in Blender (smooth/flat per primitive + sharp edges > 35°): import normals in Unity
  (`ModelImporterNormals.Import`), don't recalculate.
- FBX export: `axis_forward='-Z', axis_up='Y'`, `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS'`, `colors_type='SRGB'`,
  triangulated, no animation/leaf bones.
- Previews (`Art/Previews/`, Cycles, Standard view transform) render back faces **magenta** on purpose, to catch
  flipped winding (Unity culls back faces). `Art/Previews/_game/` holds views from roughly the battle camera
  (`VS_GAMEVIEW=1`). Card icons (transparent, 256 px) are in `Art/Icons/`.
